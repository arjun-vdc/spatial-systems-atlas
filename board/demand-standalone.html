Supply beside demand

The map measures supply: which systems provide each capability. It cannot measure demand, because it holds no use cases. The Virtual Worlds Use Case Repository of the Virtual Dimension Center links its use cases to the map's capabilities, each link resting on a sentence of the use case, and files real deployments under them. This page reads the two together. Supply figures are the map's own (6996 claims); demand is the repository's, public records only.
